Coming Back to Yourself

In a world that constantly demands we become someone else, we often lose sight of who we already are. Coming Back to Yourself is a gentle, profound exploration of returning to yourself. Through a blend of introspective wisdom, quiet observations, and mindful reflection, this book invites you to stop searching outward and start looking within.

“A quiet, transformative guide for the modern soul.”

Abhi Danoch is a writer and thinker dedicated to mindfulness, self-discovery, and the beauty of quiet reflection. He writes reflective non-fiction rooted in stillness, clarity, and honest everyday living.

About the Book

Coming Soon

In a world that constantly pulls attention outward, it is easy to lose touch with your own stillness. Coming Back to Yourself is a quiet companion for moments when your thoughts feel crowded and heavy.

Written in plain, reflective prose, it explores presence, solitude, non-attachment, and returning to what feels real and grounded.
