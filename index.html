01 / ABOUT THE AUTHOR

A little about me.

Abhi Danoch is a writer and thinker dedicated to mindfulness, self-discovery, and the beauty of quiet reflection.

He lives and works near the coast, finding inspiration in the vast, untamed natural world. Through introspective wisdom and quiet observations, his writing invites you to step back from the noise and reconnect with who you already are.
