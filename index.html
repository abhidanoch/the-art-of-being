About the Book

In a world that constantly pulls attention outward, it is easy to lose touch with your own stillness. Coming Back to Yourself is a quiet companion for moments when your thoughts feel crowded and heavy.

Written in plain, reflective prose, it explores presence, solitude, non-attachment, and returning to what feels real and grounded.
