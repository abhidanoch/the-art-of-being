About the Book

We spend so much energy trying to become someone, prove our worth, or hold on to things we cannot control that we forget how to simply be here. Coming Back to Yourself is a calm companion for moments when life feels loud and scattered.

Written in plain, honest language, it invites you to notice your thoughts without obeying every one of them, enjoy your own company without feeling lonely, and find peace in what is already enough.

About Abhi Danoch

Abhi Danoch writes reflective non-fiction focused on inner clarity, mindfulness, emotional honesty, and everyday stillness. Each piece is written to feel less like a lecture and more like a quiet conversation when the noise around you settles.
